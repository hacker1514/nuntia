Installing Nuntia

Follow the simple steps below to install Nuntia on your Android device.

1. Download the APK File

Download nuntia.apk directly to your Android device from our website or GitHub repository.

2. Temporarily Disable Play Protect (If Prompted)

Because Nuntia requests SMS permissions for offline cellular messaging, Google Play Protect may block sideloading:

A

Open Play Store Settings

Open Google Play Store → Tap Profile Picture (top right) → Select Play Protect → Tap Settings Gear.

B

Turn OFF Scanning

Turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect" temporarily.

3. Install Nuntia APK & Grant Permissions

Open your phone's Downloads or File Manager, tap nuntia.apk, and select Install. Once launched, tap "Grant" to allow SMS access and set Nuntia as your default SMS handler.

4. Re-enable Play Protect (Recommended Best Practice)

Security Recommendation: After installation completes, re-open Play Store → Play Protect → Settings → Turn "Scan apps with Play Protect" back ON. Nuntia will remain installed while your device stays protected!
