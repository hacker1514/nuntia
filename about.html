The Story Behind Nuntia

Nuntia was born from a fundamental observation: modern text messaging apps rely heavily on constant internet access, cloud servers, and data analytics. In emergency situations, remote regions, or network outages, mobile data often fails while carrier cellular SMS signals remain operational.

Nuntia was built to combine the high-class, familiar user interface of WhatsApp with 100% offline carrier cellular SMS transport. Powered by Flutter, Dart, and native Kotlin bridges, Nuntia stores all conversation histories locally on your device with zero cloud tracking.

Philosophy

Our Core Principles

1. 100% Cellular Transport

Communicates directly over carrier cell towers without requiring internet access or cloud servers.

2. Local-First Privacy

Messages and contact data reside strictly inside indexed SQLite tables on your device.

3. WhatsApp Aesthetic

Modern Emerald Teal design, dark theme, wallpapers, and fluid zero-glitch UI transitions.
