How to Install & Bypass Play Protect

Because Nuntia requires cellular SMS permissions, Google Play Protect may display a warning prompt when installing outside the Play Store. Follow these simple steps:

1

Temporarily Turn Off Play Protect

Open Google Play Store → Tap Profile Picture (top right) → Select Play Protect → Tap Settings Gear → Turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect".

2

Install Nuntia APK

Open your phone's Downloads / File Manager, tap nuntia.apk, and tap Install (or tap More Details → Install anyway).

3

Turn Play Protect Back ON (Recommended)

After Nuntia finishes installing, re-open Play Store → Play Protect → Settings → Turn "Scan apps with Play Protect" back ON for your device's ongoing security.

Recommendation: Re-enabling Play Protect after installing Nuntia keeps your smartphone 100% secure while allowing Nuntia to work uninterrupted!
